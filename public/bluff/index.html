The house rules.

01

Ten each. First out wins.

2–5 players get 10 cards each from a shuffled 52-card deck, without jokers. Unused cards are set aside. The server randomly picks who starts.

02

Play as many as you like. Claim one rank.

The leader chooses a rank, such as queens. Everyone keeps that rank until the pile clears. Play any number of cards face down, from one card to your whole hand. You can bluff with even a single card.

03

Play, pass, or call “Bluff!”

Turns move clockwise. On your turn, add cards, pass without picking up, or challenge the latest play. Even one wrong card means a bluff. The bluffer takes the entire pile and the challenger leads. If every card is true, the challenger takes the entire pile and the previous player leads.

04

All pass? The pile is gone.

If everyone else passes after the latest play, all cards in the pile leave the game. The last person who played cards leads a new round and chooses a new rank. A new play resets the passes.

05

The last cards still count.

A final play must survive a challenge or everyone passing. During this check, other players can only call bluff or pass. A caught final bluff is not a win.

Each turn has 90 seconds. If time runs out, the player passes automatically; on an empty pile, the lead moves clockwise instead. Refreshing the same browser restores your seat. Tables expire after 24 hours. Only you can see your hand.
