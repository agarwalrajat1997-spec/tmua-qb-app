Question 1 · Answer D

A 6 by 10 rectangle is cut into two equal rectangles by a straight cut parallel to its shorter sides. What is the sum of the two new perimeters?

A. 48   B. 38   C. 32   D. 44   E. 52

The original perimeter is 32. The 6-unit cut appears once on each new boundary, adding 12 for a total of 44.

Question 2 · Answer A

An isosceles triangle has sides 13,13,10. What is the distance from its apex to the midpoint of its base?

A. 12   B. 10   C. 5   D. 13   E. 8

The median from the apex bisects the base and is perpendicular to it. A right triangle has hypotenuse 13 and one leg 5, so the other leg is 12.

A wheel of radius 6 rolls without slipping through three quarters of a revolution. The distance its centre travels is kπ. What is k?

A. 18   B. 12   C. 9   D. 6   E. 8

Question 4 · Answer C

On one straight line, B lies between A and C, and C lies between B and D. If AC=11, BD=13 and AD=19, what is BC?

A. 6   B. 4   C. 5   D. 8   E. 3

AC+BD counts the whole AD once and their overlap BC once more. Thus BC=11+13−19=5.

Question 9 · Answer B

A square of area 18 is drawn with its diagonal horizontal. What is the distance between its two farthest vertices?

A. 12   B. 6   C. 9   D. 18   E. 3

In a square, diagonal squared is twice side squared. Since side squared is the area 18, the diagonal is √36=6.

Question 13 · Answer E

A rhombus has diagonals 10 and 24. A circle is tangent to all four sides. What is its radius?

A. 5   B. 120/13   C. 6   D. 4   E. 60/13

The half-diagonals form a 5–12–13 triangle, so the semiperimeter is 26. The area is 120 and equals inradius times semiperimeter. Hence r=60/13.

Question 15 · Answer A

A chord 16 units long in a circle of radius 10 lies above its centre. How far from the centre is the midpoint of the chord?

A. 6   B. 8   C. 2   D. 4   E. 10

The radius to a chord midpoint is perpendicular. Half the chord is 8, so the distance is √(10²−8²)=6.

Question 18 · Answer D

A 20 by 12 rectangle is divided by joining one corner to the midpoints of the two opposite sides. What is the area of the triangle with these three vertices?

A. 75   B. 60   C. 100   D. 90   E. 120

Take the corner at (0,0); the opposite midpoints are (20,6) and (10,12). Half the absolute determinant is (240−60)/2=90.

Question 20 · Answer C

Two equal circles of radius 5 have centres 8 units apart. What is the length of their common chord?

A. 4   B. 3   C. 6   D. 8   E. 10

The common chord bisects the line of centres perpendicularly. Its half-length is √(25−4²)=3; hence its full length is 6.

Question 22 · Answer B

A square has vertices (0,0),(8,0),(8,8),(0,8). A point P=(t,2) is equally distant from (0,0) and (8,8). What is t?

A. 5   B. 6   C. 7   D. 4   E. 2

Equate squared distances: t²+4=(8−t)²+36. Cancel t²; 16t=96, so t=6.

Question 23 · Answer E

From a point outside a circle a tangent has length 12. A secant from the same point has an exterior segment of 8. What is the length strictly inside the circle on that secant?

A. 12   B. 18   C. 8   D. 16   E. 10

Power of a point gives 12²=8 times the whole secant length, so the whole length is 18. Subtract 8 to get 10.

Question 25 · Answer D

A square’s vertices lie on a circle. Another square is made by joining the midpoints of the first. What fraction of the circle’s area is the smaller square’s area, expressed as k/π? Give k.

A. 2   B. 2/3   C. 1/2   D. 1   E. 3/2

If the circle radius is r, the first square has diagonal 2r and area 2r². Its midpoint square has half that area, r². Dividing by πr² gives 1/π.
