Question 1 · Answer C

A map is enlarged so its area increases by 69%. A road that was 20 cm long on the map becomes how many centimetres long?

A. 29   B. 23   C. 26   D. 34   E. 32

Lengths scale by the square root of the area factor. The length factor is √1.69=1.3, and 20×1.3=26.

Question 8 · Answer B

A line with positive slope passes through (2,1) and cuts the positive x-axis at x=1. What is the square of the distance from the origin to this line?

A. 1/4   B. 1/2   C. 2   D. 4   E. 1

The line through (1,0) and (2,1) is x−y−1=0. The distance is |−1|/√2, whose square is 1/2.

Question 9 · Answer E

On a cube of side 6 an ant moves from one corner to the opposite corner across exactly two adjacent faces, taking a shortest route. What is the square of its route length?

A. 108   B. 144   C. 216   D. 324   E. 180

Unfold the two faces as a 12 by 6 rectangle. The straight diagonal is shortest, with squared length 144+36=180.

Question 10 · Answer E

An equilateral triangle has side 8. A smaller equilateral triangle is formed by joining the trisection points chosen one third of the way around each side in cyclic order. What fraction of the large triangle’s area is inside the smaller?

A. 2/3   B. 1/9   C. 2/9   D. 4/9   E. 1/3

Each removed corner triangle has adjacent side lengths in the proportions 1/3 and 2/3 of the original pair with the same included angle, so each occupies 2/9 of the area. Subtract 3×2/9 from 1 to get 1/3.

Question 19 · Answer D

The diagonals of a kite are perpendicular and measure 14 and 18. An enlarged copy has perimeter twice the original. What is the area of the copy?

A. 126   B. 252   C. 378   D. 504   E. 1008

The original area is half the product of the diagonals, 126. Doubling perimeter doubles every length and quadruples area, for 504.

Question 20 · Answer A

A circular pond of radius 4 has a path of constant width around it. If the path has the same area as the pond, what is the square of the outside radius?

The total disk has area twice the pond area, or 32π. Therefore its radius squared is 32.

Question 23 · Answer A

The medians of a triangle meet at G. If the region formed by G and one entire side has area 18, what is the area of the full triangle?

A. 54   B. 60   C. 45   D. 36   E. 72

The centroid is one third of the altitude above the given base, because it lies one third of the way from the base midpoint toward the opposite vertex. The smaller triangle has one third the full area. Thus the area is 54.

Question 25 · Answer D

A rectangle is inscribed in a semicircle of radius 5 with its lower side on the diameter, and its two upper corners on the arc. If its width is 6, what is its area?

A. 20   B. 18   C. 12   D. 24   E. 30

The upper corners are symmetric, each horizontally 3 from the centre. The radius forms a 3–4–5 triangle, so height is 4. Area=6×4=24.
