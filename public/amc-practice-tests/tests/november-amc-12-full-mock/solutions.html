Question 1 · Answer B

A mixture uses syrup and water in ratio 2:7 by volume. After 6 litres of syrup are added to 27 litres of mixture, what fraction of the new mixture is syrup?

Choices: A 2/5   B 4/11   C 2/9   D 5/11   E 1/3

Initially there are 27×2/9=6 litres of syrup. The new mixture contains 12 in a total of 33 litres, hence 4/11.

Answer: B · 4/11

Question 2 · Answer B

A staircase of display cubes uses rows of 2, 5, 8, … cubes. How many cubes are in its first 12 rows together?

Choices: A 228   B 222   C 204   D 210   E 216

The last row has 2+11×3=35. Pair first and last; there are six pairs each totalling 37, so 222 cubes.

Answer: B · 222

Question 3 · Answer C

A number x satisfies log base 3 of x minus log base 3 of 4 equals 2. What is x?

Choices: A 27   B 12   C 36   D 18   E 81

Combine the logarithms to get log₃(x/4)=2. Thus x/4=9 and x=36.

Answer: C · 36

Question 4 · Answer C

A committee of 12 has mean age 30. Two members with mean age 42 leave. What is the mean age of the remaining members?

Choices: A 27   B 26   C 138/5   D 29   E 28

The total age decreases from 360 to 276. Dividing by 10 remaining members gives 138/5.

Answer: C · 138/5

Question 5 · Answer A

What is the last digit of 3²⁰²⁶ + 7²⁰²⁶?

Choices: A 8   B 4   C 2   D 6   E 0

Both last-digit cycles have length four. Since 2026≡2 mod 4, both terms end in 9; their sum ends in 8.

Answer: A · 8

Question 6 · Answer C

In a regular polygon, an interior angle is seven times its exterior angle. How many diagonals does the polygon have?

Choices: A 120   B 112   C 104   D 88   E 96

Eight exterior angles equal 180°, so one is 22.5°. There are 16 sides. Remove the 16 sides from C(16,2)=120 pairs of vertices to get 104 diagonals.

Answer: C · 104

Question 7 · Answer C

Let z=(3+i)/(1−i). What is |z|²?

Choices: A 5/2   B 2   C 5   D 10   E 8

Squared moduli multiply and divide: |3+i|²/|1−i|²=10/2=5.

Answer: C · 5

Question 8 · Answer E

A positive integer is called a split-total if it is represented by the ordered sum of three positive odd numbers. How many representations does 15 have?

Choices: A 21   B 35   C 36   D 15   E 28

Write each odd part as 2u+1. Then u+v+w=6 in nonnegative integers. Stars and bars gives C(8,2)=28.

Answer: E · 28

Question 9 · Answer C

An acute angle θ has tan θ=3/4. What is cos(2θ)?

Choices: A 4/5   B 24/25   C 7/25   D 12/25   E 3/5

Use a 3–4–5 right triangle: cosine 4/5 and sine 3/5. Their squared difference is (16−9)/25=7/25.

Answer: C · 7/25

Question 10 · Answer D

Find the exponent of 3 in 36! divided by 12!.

Choices: A 8   B 13   C 17   D 12   E 10

Legendre’s count gives 12+4+1=17 factors of 3 in 36! and 4+1=5 in 12!. The quotient contains 12.

Answer: D · 12

Question 11 · Answer D

A sequence has b₀=4 and bₙ₊₁=3bₙ−2. What is b₅?

Choices: A 486   B 729   C 972   D 730   E 243

Subtract the fixed point 1: bₙ₊₁−1=3(bₙ−1). Therefore b₅=1+3×3⁵=730.

Answer: D · 730

Question 12 · Answer B

Four fair six-sided dice are rolled. Let X be the number of different face values displayed. What is E[X]?

Choices: A 7/2   B 671/216   C 4   D 65/24   E 3

For one face the probability of appearing is 1−(5/6)⁴. Sum the six face indicators: 6[1−625/1296]=671/216.

Answer: B · 671/216

Question 13 · Answer A

Three mutually tangent circles of radii 2, 3 and 4 are externally tangent in pairs. What is the area squared of the triangle joining their centres?

Choices: A 216   B 196   C 108   D 144   E 225

The three centre distances are 5, 6 and 7. The semiperimeter is 9. Heron gives area²=9×4×3×2=216.

Answer: A · 216

Question 14 · Answer A

The positive coefficient c is chosen so x³−4x²+cx−6 has root 2. What is the sum of the squares of the other two roots, counted with multiplicity?

Choices: A -2   B 0   C 2   D 4   E 1

Substitution at 2 gives 8−16+2c−6=0, so c=7. Division by x−2 leaves x²−2x+3. Its roots have sum 2 and product 3, so the sum of their squares is 4−6=−2.

Answer: A · -2

Question 15 · Answer A

How many distinct solutions in 0 ≤ x < 2π satisfy sin(3x)=sin(x)?

Choices: A 6   B 5   C 4   D 7   E 8

Equal sines give 3x=x+2kπ or 3x=π−x+2kπ. The first gives two multiples of π; the second gives four odd multiples of π/4. The lists are disjoint, for 6.

Answer: A · 6

Question 16 · Answer E

Eight different letters include A, B and C. In how many permutations do A and B occur next to each other while B and C do not?

Choices: A 7200   B 11520   C 7920   D 10080   E 8640

Contract AB or BA: 2×7! permutations. With both adjacencies, B is in the middle of ABC or CBA, giving 2×6!. Subtract to get 8640.

Answer: E · 8640

Question 17 · Answer B

A positive integer in base b is written 121; in base b+2 it is written 81. What is its decimal value?

Choices: A 121   B 81   C 144   D 100   E 64

The two expressions are (b+1)² and 8b+17. Equating gives (b−8)(b+2)=0. Only b=8 is a valid base; 121 in base 8 is 64+16+1=81.

Answer: B · 81

Question 18 · Answer E

A point (t,0) on the x-axis is joined by line segments to (1,4) and (8,3). What is the square of the least possible total length?

Choices: A 74   B 81   C 100   D 50   E 98

Reflect (1,4) to (1,−4). The two segments then form a broken line no shorter than the straight line to (8,3). That segment crosses the axis, so equality occurs. Its squared length is 7²+7²=98.

Answer: E · 98

Question 19 · Answer B

The polynomial x⁴+ax²+16 has four distinct real roots that are all integers. What is the sum of all possible values of |a|?

Choices: A 8   B 17   C 20   D 16   E 25

Roots occur as ±u, ±v with distinct positive u,v. Their squares multiply to 16, so uv=4 and the only distinct pair is 1,4. Thus a=−(1+16)=−17, giving 17.

Answer: B · 17

Question 20 · Answer B

A point is uniformly distributed in the unit square. What is the probability that its distances to the two coordinate axes have sum less than 1 and differ by less than 1/2?

Choices: A 1/4   B 3/8   C 7/16   D 5/16   E 1/2

The triangle x+y<1 has area 1/2. The line x−y=1/2 cuts off a triangle with base 1/2 on the x-axis and height 1/4, of area 1/16. The corresponding y-side triangle is congruent. Subtract twice 1/16 to obtain 3/8.

Answer: B · 3/8

Question 21 · Answer E

Define f(x)=(x−1)/(x+1). Starting with x=3, apply f exactly 2026 times. What is the absolute value of the result?

Choices: A 2   B 2/3   C 3   D 1/2   E 1/3

The first four values after starting are 1/2, −1/3, −2, 3. They form a cycle of length four. Since 2026≡2 mod 4, the result is −1/3, whose absolute value is 1/3.

Answer: E · 1/3

Question 22 · Answer A

How many different circular necklaces have eight beads, exactly four black and four white, if rotations are identified but mirror images are not identified unless a rotation matches them?

Choices: A 10   B 8   C 12   D 9   E 14

Average the fixed colourings over the eight rotations. Identity fixes 70; the half-turn fixes C(4,2)=6. Each quarter-turn fixes 2; odd turns fix none. Thus (70+6+2+2)/8=10.

Answer: A · 10

Question 23 · Answer D

What is the sum of gcd(k,30) over 1 ≤ k ≤ 30?

Choices: A 150   B 180   C 105   D 135   E 120

For each divisor d of 30 the count of k with gcd=d is φ(30/d). For d=1,2,3,5,6,10,15,30 the contributions are 8,16,12,10,24,20,15,30 respectively. Their sum is 135.

Answer: D · 135

Question 24 · Answer D

Let ω be a nonreal cube root of unity. Compute the squared modulus of (1−ω)⁵/(1−ω²)².

Choices: A 81   B 18   C 3   D 27   E 9

The two nonreal cube roots are conjugates. Each squared distance to 1 is (1+1/2)²+3/4=3. The squared modulus of the quotient is 3⁵/3²=27.

Answer: D · 27

Question 25 · Answer E

Select a subset of {1,2,…,30} in which no chosen number is twice another chosen number. What is the largest possible size?

Choices: A 18   B 19   C 15   D 21   E 20

Partition by odd part d into doubling chains d,2d,4d,… . On a chain of length L, at most ⌈L/2⌉ can be chosen, attained by alternating entries. The chain lengths for odd d=1,3,…,29 are 5,4,3,3,2,2,2,2,1,1,1,1,1,1,1. Their alternating maxima sum to 20.

Answer: E · 20
