These are different assessment systems, so this bank should carry an explicit first-assessment 2027 label. Shared questions should be stored once and displayed at both levels. ## Assessment and bank styles

Both levels take a 90-minute, 35-mark Paper 1 and a 90-minute, 35-mark Paper 2. Paper 1 has two 4-mark content/example questions, two 6-mark applications to supplied scenarios, and a choice of one 15-mark conceptual response from two contexts. The first section has no specified context. Specific memorized study details are not assessed in its content questions or extended response. [Public guide, printed 42–46](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/subject-guides/psychology-first-assessment-2027-guide-sbs.pdf), [examiner paper 1 requirements](https://ibpublishing.ibo.org/exinst/apps/exinst/index.html?chapter=9&doc=EX_instructions_2027_e&part=10).

Paper 2 A uses the student’s class-practical experience: knowledge 4, concept application 4, comparison 6 and design 6. The assessed focus is methodology. A self-contained bank item supplying a fictitious practical record must identify this as a practice adaptation. Paper 2 B supplies an unseen study, specifies at least four concepts and asks students to discuss at least two for 15 marks. [Guide, printed 47–49](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/subject-guides/psychology-first-assessment-2027-guide-sbs.pdf), [examiner paper 2 requirements](https://ibpublishing.ibo.org/exinst/apps/exinst/index.html?chapter=9&doc=EX_instructions_2027_e&part=10).

HL adds a 105-minute, 30-mark Paper 3: graph-interpretation limitation 3; findings/conclusion 6; qualitative credibility, bias or transferability 6; claim synthesis 15. The last task uses at least three supplied sources and relevant own knowledge. Sources combine quantitative and qualitative research within one extension and one context. Synthetic data are permitted but must be labelled accurately. [Guide, printed 49–52](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/subject-guides/psychology-first-assessment-2027-guide-sbs.pdf).

New-course marking uses holistic best-fit bands and indicative content. An exemplar answer is one valid route, not a checklist of compulsory one-mark facts. Original rubric wording should preserve the score ranges: 4 marks uses 0/1–2/3–4; 6 marks adds 5–6; 15 marks uses 0/1–3/4–6/7–9/10–12/13–15. The 3-mark graph task develops identification into explanation. Command terms distinguish description, application, analysis, comparison and evaluation. Paper 1 C uses evaluate, discuss, examine or to what extent. [Guide, printed 44–52, 61–62](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/subject-guides/psychology-first-assessment-2027-guide-sbs.pdf).

No current calculator permission was established from the accessed primary documents. ## Scope and classification

All students study biological, cognitive and sociocultural content, research methodology, six concepts, and all four contexts. The concepts are bias, causality, change, measurement, perspective and responsibility. Contexts are health/well-being, human development, human relationships and learning/cognition. Context subheadings are organizational, while selected disorders, processes and theories allow choice; every named example is not mandatory recall. See the complete factual vocabulary in specification.json and topic-taxonomy.json. BIO/COG/SOC/RES/CUL/MOT/TEC codes are internal navigation codes. [Guide, printed 18–34](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/subject-guides/psychology-first-assessment-2027-guide-sbs.pdf).

Animal models are shared 2027 biological content. The HL additions are deeper inquiry into culture, motivation and technology, together with directly assessed data interpretation. Data-analysis material is studied at both levels but is not directly assessed at SL. Shared bank graphics should consequently support conceptual mechanisms or methodology rather than ask SL students to interpret quantitative findings. [Guide, printed 19, 27, 36–39](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/subject-guides/psychology-first-assessment-2027-guide-sbs.pdf).

The practical pairing is fixed: health interview; development observation; relationships survey; learning experiment (true or quasi). Minimum student-led samples are one individual interview or 3–8 focus-group participants, one observed participant, ten survey participants, and five experimental participants. Particular activities remain choices. The new IA is a 24-mark research proposal, not a completed experiment. Its weights are 30% SL / 20% HL; external weights are 35/35 at SL and 25/25/30 at HL. [IB curriculum update](https://www.ibo.org/university-admission/latest-curriculum-updates/psychology-updates/), [2027 brief](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/programmes/dp/pdfs/dp_psychology_subjectbrief_en.pdf).

## Review traps

Every question needs a separate scientific and assessment review. Check the precise mechanism, causal limitations, alternative explanations, concept links, exemplar and best-fit rubric. For figures, record actual coordinates, labels, direction and meaning; inspect at mobile width. Synthetic results or quotations must never be attributed to named real studies.

Distinguish random allocation from random sampling, reliability from validity, qualitative transferability from simple population generalization, and correlation from causation. A national cultural average does not determine an individual; inherited vulnerability does not imply inevitability. Imaging modality matters: structural MRI, fMRI and PET support different inferences. Neurotransmitter activity is not a universal one-chemical explanation of a disorder. In HL data, verify every sample size, scale direction, quartile, summary statistic, error-bar definition and inferential claim. Difficulty should arise from inference and argument, not memorized publication years.

The completed-experiment IA, 9-mark SAQs and 22-mark ERQs belong to the legacy course. Legacy timings are Paper 1 two hours/49 marks at both levels; Paper 2 one hour/22 marks at SL and two hours/44 marks at HL; old HL Paper 3 one hour/24 marks. They should not be mixed into this bank. [Legacy guide, printed 44–58](https://xmltwo.ibo.org/publications/EAD/2026_EN/GR3/GR3-Psy_EN.pdf), [legacy examiner instructions](https://ibpublishing.ibo.org/exinst/apps/exinst/index.html?chapter=9&doc=EX_instructions_2026_e&part=10).
