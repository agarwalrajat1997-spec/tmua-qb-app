# Business management authoring blueprint

Checked 31 October 2026. This bank is independent, original practice. All businesses, survey results, financial statements and correspondence in its questions are fictional teaching data.

## Primary-source decisions

- [Current guide, February 2022, updated July 2024](https://xmltwo.ibo.org/publications/EAD/2026_EN/GR3/GR3-BM_EN_24.pdf), accessed through the current examiner instructions: first assessment 2024, especially printed pages 13–15, 21–35, 39–50 and 64–66.
- [Official SL subject brief](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/programmes/dp/pdfs/business-management-sl-subject-brief-en.pdf) and [HL subject brief](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/programmes/dp/pdfs/business-management-hl-subject-brief-en.pdf): checked curriculum, assessment and the specimen-derived sample questions. The complete specimen pack was not available through the public sample-exam page; no third-party paper was copied or used as a question source.
- [2026 examiner instructions](https://ibpublishing.ibo.org/exinst/apps/exinst/index.html?chapter=1&doc=EX_instructions_2026_e&part=10) and [2027 examiner instructions](https://ibpublishing.ibo.org/exinst/apps/exinst/index.html?chapter=1&doc=EX_instructions_2027_e&part=10), checked for analytical marking, own-figure calculations and current Paper 3 clarifications. The bank follows the 2027 clarifications where these differ.

The public business-management landing page still describes the older course despite its recent timestamp. It was rejected as a syllabus authority. Contemporary concepts are change, creativity, ethics and sustainability. There are no CUEGIS-format questions.

## Coverage and assessment

There are 20 explicitly identified families per unit, four case variants per family: 400 records in total. Planned SL/AHL split is 68/12, 44/36, 52/28, 56/24 and 40/40, totalling 260/140. The level follows the highest demand in each record. All Unit 1 syllabus content is shared; AHL labels there require HL toolkit demand or the HL-only Paper 3 task.

Codes follow the current guide: Unit 1 has 1.1–1.6; Unit 2 has 2.1–2.7; Unit 3 has 3.1–3.9; Unit 4 has 4.1–4.6; Unit 5 has 5.1–5.9. Break-even is 5.5. All seven marketing Ps are shared; international marketing and sales forecasting are HL. Recruitment, appraisal, labour turnover and the additional motivation theories are HL. Straight-line and units-of-production depreciation, efficiency ratios, NPV and budgets are HL.

Shared toolkit includes SWOT, Ansoff, STEEPLE, BCG, business plans, decision trees, descriptive statistics and circular business models. HL toolkit includes force field analysis, Gantt charts, Hofstede, Porter, contribution costing, critical path analysis and simple linear regression. Ordinary break-even contribution per unit remains shared 5.5.

Paper 1 is common SL/HL, excludes HL extensions, and totals 30 marks in 90 minutes. Paper 2 is quantitatively focused: SL 40 marks/90 minutes, HL 50 marks/105 minutes. HL Paper 3 is 25 marks/75 minutes, with 2-, 6- and 17-mark tasks about a social enterprise. Bank items are targeted practice sets, not purported complete examination papers. Every item permits a calculator; the guide states four-function calculators suffice and GDCs are allowed subject to annual calculator rules.

Two- to six-mark explanations use distinct analytical criteria with explicit business consequences and case application. Ten-mark responses use independently written best-fit bands plus case-specific indicative material; they are not ten-point checklists. Paper 1 theoretical-only responses are capped at 2, Paper 2 at 4; the highest ten-mark band requires explained limitations. Prompts asking for a recommendation require a justified decision. Alternative defensible judgments receive equal credit.

The 17-mark Paper 3 plan has separate best-fit criteria A resources (4), B tools/theory (4), C evaluation (6), D sequencing/plan (3). Under 2027 instructions, implied resource use is accepted; a toolkit tool OR relevant theory/content can meet the threshold for 2 in B. A separate conclusion is not compulsory. Plans must consider at least two functional areas and cross-functional trade-offs.

Every record receives a scope, calculation/reasoning, marking and source-originality ledger entry with a content hash. Quantitative inputs have an explicit period, currency or unit, appropriate rounding and sufficient data. SVGs are original self-contained illustrations and receive geometry checks and rendered visual inspection.
